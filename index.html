FAQ

Before you book.

How do I book a slot? +

Go to Book Ground, choose your sport and date, tap any green (available) slot, fill in your details, and confirm on WhatsApp.

How do I pay? +

After you confirm on WhatsApp, the admin sends Easypaisa/JazzCash details. Pay and send the screenshot to lock the slot.

Can I cancel? +

Yes — message us on WhatsApp at least 2 hours before your slot for a full refund or credit.

Can I reschedule? +

Yes, subject to availability on your new preferred date and hour. Ask the admin over WhatsApp.
